About Vendorfy

A marketplace that connects shoppers with independent vendors across Nigeria.

What Vendorfy does

Vendorfy is an online marketplace. Vendors open a store, list their products and set their own prices. Shoppers browse everything in one place, order in a few minutes and pay when the order arrives at their door.

We do not hold stock ourselves. Each product is sold and delivered by the vendor who listed it, and Vendorfy gives both sides a simple, safe place to do it.

Who it is for

Shoppers

Anyone in Nigeria who wants to buy fashion, beauty, electronics, home and kitchen items, baby products, groceries and more from sellers they can reach, and who prefers to pay once the order is in their hands.

Vendors

Small and growing businesses that want an online shop without building a website. You list your products, set your prices, see your orders and deliver. We bring the shoppers.

Open your store

How it works

  • A shopper creates a free account with just a name and an email. We send a one-time code, so there is no password to remember or lose.
  • They add products to the cart and place an order with their delivery details.
  • The vendor sees the order, ships it, and marks it delivered. The shopper pays on delivery.
  • Until an item ships, the shopper can cancel it and the stock goes back on the shelf.

Vendors are checked

A new vendor store is reviewed by our team before its products show in the shop. This helps keep the marketplace trustworthy for shoppers.
