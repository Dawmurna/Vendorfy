Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated:

This policy explains how (trading as Vendorfy, "we", "us") handles your personal information when you use . We try to collect as little as we need, and we do not sell your information.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name and email address.
  • Your delivery details: name, phone number, address, city and state, when you save them or place an order.
  • Your activity: your cart, wishlist and orders, so your account remembers them.
  • If you are a vendor: your store name, category, contact details and the products and photos you upload.
  • Basic technical data: things our server sees when you visit, such as your IP address, used to keep the site secure and to limit abuse.

How we use it

  • To create your account and send you the one-time code you use to log in.
  • To process your orders and pass the delivery details to the vendor who needs them.
  • To show you your cart, wishlist and order history.
  • To keep the site safe, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

Who sees your information

  • Vendors see your name, phone number, delivery address and any note you add, but only for the items in your order that they sell. They need it to deliver.
  • Service providers that help us run the site, such as our hosting and email sending services, handle data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to share information.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Cookies and storage

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. It is set by our server and cannot be read by scripts on the page. We also store your cart and wishlist in your browser while you are not logged in, so they are not lost. We do not use advertising cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order history while your account is active and as long as we need it for records, disputes and legal obligations. Login codes expire after 10 minutes and are deleted.

How we protect it

Login codes are stored in scrambled form, accounts can only see their own data, and the site is served over a secure connection. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us.

Your choices and rights

You can see and update your name and delivery details on your account page. You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete your account by emailing . We will respond within a reasonable time. Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act applies, you also have the rights it gives you.

Children

Vendorfy is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If the change is important we will tell you on the site.
